Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what My World Flashcards collects when a parent or guardian orders personalized toddler flashcards, why we collect it, and how to ask us to delete it. The contact email is hello@myworldflashcards.com.

Who this is for

The website is for parents and guardians. A child does not create an account, and we do not ask a child to send us information. A parent or guardian submits the order.

Information we collect

When you place an order, the form asks for:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your child's first name and age
  • Notes about who is in the photos, routines, favorite things, and the style you want
  • The package you selected
  • Photos you choose to upload (a photo of your child, plus optional family, routine, and extra photos)
  • An order reference created in your browser so we can match the form to the payment

If you email us, we keep that conversation so we can reply. We do not ask for a phone number.

Why we collect it

We use this information to make the flashcards you ordered, to reply about the order, to take payment, and to handle a refund or a deletion request. Photos are used only to make that order. We do not sell photos or contact details. We do not use your photos in advertising, and we do not give them to other customers.

Photos and Basin

The order form sends your details and photo files to Basin, the form service we use to receive orders. Basin stores that submission so we can open the files, make the cards, and write back to you. We do not receive the photos through any other upload on this website. Please upload a photo only if you have permission to share it for this order.

Payments

Package payments are processed by Stripe. You enter card details on Stripe's page, not on ours. My World Flashcards does not receive or store your full card number, expiration date, or security code. We may see that a payment succeeded, the amount, and the email address on the payment so we can match it to your order.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact details, your child's first name and age, your notes, and your photos while we make and deliver the order, and for a short time afterward in case you need a correction or support. Payment records may be kept longer when tax or accounting rules require it.

You can email hello@myworldflashcards.com and ask us to delete your photos and order details. We will delete them unless a limited record has to stay for a legal or accounting reason, such as the fact that a payment was made. When you ask, we will also delete the Basin submission that holds your files, or ask Basin to delete it.

Children's privacy

We follow a simple rule: a parent or guardian sends the information, not the child. The child's first name, age, and photos are there so we can make that child's cards. We do not build a profile for advertising, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from a child. If you believe a child sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Who else sees it

We share order information only with the services that help us complete an order: Basin for the form and file upload, Stripe for payment, and email so we can reply to you. We do not sell personal information.
