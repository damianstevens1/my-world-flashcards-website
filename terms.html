The service

We make a custom digital sample or a private flashcard app from the photos and notes you send. The sample app on this website is a demo. It is not your child's finished order. The cards are a family learning aid. They are not medical care, speech therapy, or a diagnosis.

Packages and prices

The current packages are:

  • Sample Preview, $15. About 3 personalized sample practice pages.
  • Mini Personalized App, $49. A private personalized app with about 12-15 practice pages.

The price is the price shown for that package when you submit the form. These are custom digital products. Page counts are approximate and depend on the photos and notes you send.

Orders and payment

We do not store your card number. We start making the cards after we have both the photos and the payment. First previews are usually ready within 24-48 hours after payment.

When we deliver the sample or app, you may use it for your family's personal learning. Please don't resell the files, the app, or the artwork. Don't upload photos you don't have permission to share.
